UNDERSTAND THE TERMS

Common Terms & Definitions

Throughout this website you may see some terms you are not familiar with. Here is a few terms and definitions to help you as you explore our site:

Community Garden: A garden shared by members of a community, usually in an urban setting. Typically, they are fenced off to keep out deer, but anyone who is a part of a community garden has a key to get in. These spaces are maintained by members of the community or local neighborhood.

Urban Farm: This can refer to various practices of cultivating, processing, and distributing food across urban areas. This allows members of a neighborhood or community to have more autonomy over their food system, and provides a sense of security for many involved. Depending on the size of the area you are working with and location exact practices may vary. Popular forms of Urban farming include hydroponics, beekeeping, CSA farming, and other forms of horticulture.
