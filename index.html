UNDERSTAND THE TERMS

Common Terms & Definitions

Throughout this website you may see some terms you are not familiar with. Here are a few terms and definitions to help you as you explore our site:

01

Community Garden

A garden shared by members of a community, usually in an urban setting. Typically, they are fenced off to keep out deer, and members of the community garden have a key to get in. These spaces are maintained by members of the community or local neighborhood.

02

Urban Farm

Cultivating, processing, and distributing food across urban areas so neighborhoods have greater autonomy over their food system and a stronger sense of food security. Depending on space and location, practices often include hydroponics, beekeeping, CSA farming, and greenhouse growing.

03

CSA Farming

CSA stands for Community Supported Agriculture. A farmer offers a set number of “shares” (typically weekly boxes of produce) to the community. Farmers gain reliable support early in the season, while shareholders receive fresh seasonal produce and connect directly with their local food system.

04

SNAP

SNAP stands for Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program. This is a federally funded program that helps lower-income families purchase eligible groceries. Applications go through your local assistance office, and benefit amounts depend on household size, income, and expenses.

05

EBT

EBT stands for Electronic Benefit Transfer, a payment system that lets you buy groceries with your SNAP benefits using a plastic card loaded at the start of each month like a debit card. You can find participating retailers on our Local Grocery Stores tab.

06

HIP

HIP stands for the Massachusetts Healthy Incentives Program. It matches your SNAP dollars automatically when you buy eligible fresh fruits and vegetables from participating local farmers markets, farm stands, mobile markets, and CSA programs, stretching your food budget further.
