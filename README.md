# Massachusetts Community Food Directory (`massfoodinsecurity`)

Created by undergraduate students at Olin College of Engineering and Babson College for their final project in **Bioengineering and Beyond!**. This directory promotes food sovereignty and preventative healthcare by making Massachusetts food assistance programs, community gardens, urban farms, and local grocers easier to discover and access.

## Pages & Features

* **Home (`index.html`):** Introduces the project's mission and features a category-filterable timeline of Massachusetts food security news, policy updates, community actions, and research reports.
* **Background (`background.html`):** Explores the health impacts of nutrition, systemic factors contributing to food deserts, and CDC nutrition and food access programs.
* **Community Gardens (`gardens.html`):** Provides an interactive Leaflet and OpenStreetMap directory of 111 community gardens and 8 urban farms across Boston neighborhoods, searchable by ZIP code and filterable by space type.
* **Local Grocery Stores (`groceries.html`):** Highlights affordable local grocery stores, farmers markets, and food pantries alongside SNAP/EBT and Healthy Incentives Program (HIP) details.
* **About Us (`about.html`):** Shares the project statement and introduces the student team.

## Built With

* HTML, CSS, and JavaScript (`css/style.css`, `js/script.js`)
* Leaflet and OpenStreetMap for interactive garden and urban farm mapping
