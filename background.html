UNDERSTANDING FOOD ASSISTANCE

Background & Food Assistance

Understanding state and federal food assistance programs empowers individuals and community organizers to maximize access to fresh, healthy, and affordable food.

SNAP EBT Benefits

Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program

The Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) helps eligible households purchase groceries through monthly benefits deposited onto an Electronic Benefits Transfer (EBT) card.

SNAP benefits can be used at participating grocery stores, farmers markets, and other approved food retailers.

How SNAP can help

  • Provides monthly funds for eligible food purchases.
  • Can be used at participating grocery stores and farmers markets.
  • May connect qualifying households with additional assistance programs.
  • Benefits are deposited onto an EBT card.

HIP Cashback Program

Massachusetts Healthy Incentives Program

The Healthy Incentives Program (HIP) gives eligible SNAP households money back on their EBT card when they purchase qualifying locally grown fruits and vegetables from participating farm vendors.

The incentive is automatically returned to the household's EBT account when an eligible purchase is made.

Monthly household incentives

  • 1–2 Person Household Up to $40
  • 3–5 Person Household Up to $60
  • 6+ Person Household Up to $80

Incentive amounts and program rules can change. Check the official Massachusetts website for current limits and eligibility.
